# 108-Day Experiment Constitution

**Status:** Active experiment specification\
**Experiment period:** 7 September 2026 -- 23 December 2026\
**Duration:** 108 days\
**Primary financial goal:** ₹3,00,000 per month\
**Starting weight:** 87.7 kg\
**Target weight:** 75 kg

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 1. Purpose

This document is the authoritative constitution for the 108-Day Project.

The experiment is designed to observe what happens when the defined
daily protocol is executed consistently for 108 days, while recording
changes in:

1.  Protocol execution
2.  Health and physical condition
3.  Mental/internal state
4.  Focused and valuable work
5.  Financial outcomes
6.  Opportunities and observable life events

The experiment is intended to generate evidence. It must not be designed
to assume that the hypothesis is true.

The application and all supporting systems must preserve the distinction
between:

**protocol → internal state → behavior → opportunities → financial
outcome**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 2. Primary Goal

The financial goal statement used throughout the experiment is:

> **I create 3 lakh per month through focused, valuable work that I
> consistently execute.**

This is the goal used for the 3-6-9 practice.

The goal is not merely to repeat the statement. The practice is intended
to help internalize and feel the achieved-goal state, refine its
emotional signature, maintain focus, and guide behavior.

The experiment does not assume that repetition itself causes the
financial outcome.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 3. Core Hypothesis

### H1 --- Protocol and financial outcome

Consistent execution of the 108-day protocol will materially improve
financial outcomes.

### Stretch hypothesis

Consistent execution of the 108-day protocol will enable achievement of
the financial target of **₹3,00,000 per month**.

The stretch hypothesis is an outcome target, not a guarantee.

### H2 --- Internal state, behavior and execution

Repeatedly entering and internalizing the psychological state associated
with already achieving the financial goal will improve focus, behavior,
opportunity recognition, and execution.

### H3 --- Cross-domain effects

Consistent execution of the protocol will produce improvements in
health, discipline, mental state, work behavior, and other measurable
areas that may be interconnected with financial outcomes.

### Exploratory H4 --- Beyond observable mechanisms

Financial improvement may occur beyond what can reasonably be explained
by observable changes in work, behavior, opportunity generation, and
execution.

H4 is exploratory. The experiment must be capable of failing to support
it.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 4. Experimental Principle

The experiment records what happens.

It does not begin with the conclusion:

> "Manifestation works."

It asks:

> **What evidence appears after 108 days of consistent execution?**

At the end of the experiment, hypotheses may be classified as:

-   Supported
-   Partially supported
-   Inconclusive
-   Not supported

The final assessment must include evidence, alternative explanations,
limitations, and confidence.

## 7. Successful-Day Definition

A successful day is determined by the protocol-scoring rules below.

### Days 1--46 --- Definition v1

**13 / 13 conditions are required.**

Every one of the 13 prescribed conditions must be completed for the day
to be successful.

Days 1--46 remain scored under Definition v1.

**They must never be recalculated under the later threshold.**

### Days 47--108 --- Current threshold

**At least 8 / 13 conditions are required.**

A day with 8 or more completed conditions is successful under the
current threshold.

### Important historical-integrity rule

The scoring framework is versioned.

A later scoring definition must not retroactively change the historical
classification of Days 1--46.

For example, if a Day 30 record contains 10/13 completed conditions, it
remains **not successful** under Definition v1. It must not later become
successful because the Days 47--108 threshold is ≥8/13.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 8. What Successful-Day Status Means

Successful-day status measures **protocol execution only**.

It does not claim that:

-   the entire day was productive;
-   meaningful work was completed;
-   money was earned;
-   the hypothesis was supported;
-   the desired internal state was achieved.

A successful day means that the applicable protocol-scoring definition
was satisfied.

The experiment may separately record what happened during the remainder
of the day.

## 11. Observation vs Interpretation

The experiment must distinguish facts from interpretations.

### Observation

A directly recorded event or measurement.

Example:

> Received four inbound enquiries during the week.

### Interpretation

A possible explanation.

Example:

> Increased confidence may have changed networking or outreach behavior.

Interpretations must not be stored or presented as established facts.

Where useful, record confidence in the interpretation.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 12. Evidence Standard

Evidence must be recorded in a way that allows both supporting and
challenging evidence to survive.

The experiment must explicitly track:

### Evidence supporting a hypothesis

Observed changes or patterns consistent with the hypothesis.

### Evidence challenging a hypothesis

Observed results inconsistent with the hypothesis.

### Alternative explanations

Other plausible reasons for an observed outcome.

Examples of alternative explanations may include:

-   increased work volume;
-   a new business opportunity;
-   changes in market conditions;
-   changes in pricing;
-   networking;
-   prior business activity;
-   random variation;
-   seasonal effects;
-   health or schedule changes.

The experiment must not selectively record only positive evidence.

## 15. Experiment Integrity Rules

### Rule 1 --- No retroactive rewriting

Historical observations, measurements, and daily classifications must
not be silently changed.

### Rule 2 --- Version scoring rules

If the successful-day definition changes, the new definition receives a
new version.

Historical days retain the definition under which they were originally
scored.

### Rule 3 --- Protocol amendments are explicit

If the protocol changes after the experiment begins:

1.  Record the amendment.
2.  Record the date.
3.  Record the reason.
4.  Preserve the previous definition.
5.  Do not silently rewrite historical records.

### Rule 4 --- Missing data is missing

Unknown values must remain unknown.

Do not treat missing data as zero, failure, success, or evidence.

### Rule 5 --- Observation is not explanation

A measured outcome and an explanation for that outcome must remain
distinguishable.

### Rule 6 --- Correlation is not causation

A relationship between two variables must not automatically be described
as causal.

### Rule 7 --- Preserve negative evidence

Evidence that challenges a hypothesis is as important as evidence
supporting it.

### Rule 8 --- No conclusion before the evidence

The system may summarize observations during the experiment, but final
hypothesis assessment belongs to the end-of-experiment analysis.
