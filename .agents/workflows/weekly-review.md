# Weekly Review Workflow

## Purpose
Review the latest experiment execution and evidence without turning the review into confirmation of the hypothesis.

## Flow
Collect observations
→ Validate missingness
→ Protocol review
→ Health/mind/work/wealth review
→ Opportunities
→ Trends/relationships
→ Supporting/challenging evidence
→ Alternative explanations
→ Actions
→ Record review

## Steps

1. Establish the exact seven-day period being reviewed.
2. Collect recorded protocol results without filling missing values.
3. Review successful-day scoring using the definition applicable to each day.
4. Review 3-6-9 completion separately from outcome achievement.
5. Review health, mind, work, wealth, and opportunity observations.
6. Identify trends and relationships without asserting causation.
7. Record supporting, challenging, neutral, and missing evidence.
8. Consider plausible alternative explanations.
9. Identify practical actions for the following week without changing the experiment silently.
10. Record the review as analysis, not as a replacement for raw observations.

## Rules
- Do not cherry-pick favorable evidence.
- Do not convert correlation into causation.
- Do not rewrite historical observations.
- Clearly distinguish observations from interpretations.
