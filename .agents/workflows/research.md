# Research Workflow

## Purpose
Perform documented research before making decisions involving unfamiliar or changing technology, scientific methods, or external information.

## Flow
Research question
→ Primary sources
→ Verification
→ Facts/assumptions
→ Recommendation
→ Research record
→ Decision/plan

## Steps

1. Define the precise question.
2. Identify authoritative sources.
3. Verify current behavior and constraints.
4. Separate facts, recommendations, and assumptions.
5. Record important findings under `docs/research/`.
6. Identify alternatives and risks.
7. Return a recommendation.
8. If the result changes architecture or product behavior, route the decision through the appropriate planning/ADR process.

## Rules
- Research does not itself authorize implementation.
- Prefer primary sources.
- Do not fabricate citations or capabilities.
